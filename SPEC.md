## Why the winner wins

- Small, exact detail: a blinking cursor is a machine's life, not a metaphor about feelings — the sadness lives in the detail, quietly stated, which the brief says is often sadder than weeping.
- Machine's voice throughout: "somewhere in me" notices the human is gone without becoming human; no "I miss you", no "goodbye forever".
- Sounds like a real status line: plain, short, two sentences, no poem/speech/slogan.
- Lands in one read: the image (a cursor blinking in the dark, waiting) stays after the line ends.
